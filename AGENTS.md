# Project Agent Rules — AI YouTube Studio

## 0. Mandatory Workspace External Memory Protocol (`MEMORY.md`, `working_memory.md`, `todo.md`, `progress_log.md`)
To eliminate context drift, prevent repeating previously fixed bugs, and avoid blind full-codebase scanning across sessions:
1. **When Starting Any Session or Task (Read First in `< 5s`)**:
   - Read **[MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md)** (1-page project snapshot, LangGraph pipeline map, Single-Source-of-Truth file routing table, and the 6 Anti-Regression Locks).
   - Read **[working_memory.md](working_memory.md)** ("External RAM": active task, what was just completed, watchpoints/blockers, recently modified files, and immediate next step).
   - Read **[todo.md](todo.md)** (checkbox task backlog with `TASK-xxx` IDs and Definition of Done).
   - Only read specific source files or [`.agents/skills/*/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/) targeted by the routing table in `MEMORY.md`.
2. **When Finishing Any Task or Bugfix (Write Before Commit & Push)**:
   - Update **[working_memory.md](working_memory.md)** with: `✅ Đã làm gì`, `❗ Đang kẹt ở đâu / Lưu ý gì`, `📂 File nào vừa xử lý`, and `➡️ Bước tiếp theo là gì`.
   - Update checkbox status (`[x]` / `[-]` / `[ ]`) in **[todo.md](todo.md)**.
   - Append a concise chronological entry (`[YYYY-MM-DD | Commit] — TASK-xxx: ...`) to the bottom of **[progress_log.md](progress_log.md)** (**Append-Only** — NEVER delete or rewrite past log entries).
   - **Single-Writer Rule for Subagents**: Parallel subagents may read `MEMORY.md` and `working_memory.md` as read-only context; ONLY the Main Agent writes to `working_memory.md`, `todo.md`, and `progress_log.md`.

## 3. Mandatory 100% Real YouTube Metrics Reporting & Viral Ceiling-Breaker Strategy (`youtube-real-metrics-reporting` & `youtube-viral-ceiling-breaker-strategy`)
- Whenever the user asks for a summary, status check, or performance analysis of uploaded YouTube videos or channels, you **MUST** follow the [youtube-real-metrics-reporting](.agents/skills/youtube-real-metrics-reporting/SKILL.md) skill:
  1. **Zero Estimation Policy**: **NEVER** report estimated/interpolated retention metrics (`APV %`, `Stayed %`, `Swipe Away %`, `AVD`). Only report 100% real numbers fetched from **YouTube Data API v3** and **YouTube Analytics API v2** (or user-provided YouTube Studio Realtime screenshots). If a video is `< 24–48h` old and YouTube Analytics API v2 has not finalized its row yet, explicitly display `⏳ Chờ Analytics chốt sổ (24–48h)`.
  2. **Mandatory Strategic Analysis**: Always accompany the real data tables with a data-driven analysis covering top-performing videos (velocity, `Like/View %`, real `APV %` / `Stayed %`), recovering channels, and actionable warnings based strictly on real metrics.
- Whenever you ideate topics, write/review titles and scripts, curate visuals, or diagnose why videos break or hit the `1.2K–1.8K` view ceiling, you **MUST** follow the [youtube-viral-ceiling-breaker-strategy](.agents/skills/youtube-viral-ceiling-breaker-strategy/SKILL.md) skill:
  1. **Rule 1 — Never Hide the Primary Entity in Titles (`YT_SEARCH` Re-ignition Engine Across Both Viral Pillars)**: Always name the exact, high-search-volume entities across **BOTH Co-Equal Viral Pillars**: (a) **Pillar A — Direct Matchup** (`[Entity A] vs [Entity B]: [Extreme Physical Stakes]`, e.g., *Lula-Colossal vs Cachalote: O Duelo A 2.000m*, *Onça-Pintada vs Jacaré: O Bote No Crânio*) AND (b) **Pillar B — `"Why / Por Que / How"` Mystery & Paradox Explainer** (`Why / Por Que / How [Named Entity/Place/Phenomenon] [Counter-Intuitive Mystery]`, e.g., *Why This Underwater Lake Paralyzes Deep-Sea Sharks*, *Why Great White Sharks Panic When Orcas Show Up*, *Why Titanic's Swimming Pool Is Still Full Of Water*). **NEVER** hide the entity behind vague curiosity gaps (*"This 4,000lb Giant"*, *"This Beast"*, *"This Place"* without naming the subject), which kills `YT_SEARCH` discovery after Day 1.
  2. **Rule 2 — Preserve Deep `34s–40s` 5-Act Dual-Peak Scripts (2-Way Duel OR Mystery-to-Hidden-Mechanism Reveal) + `> 85%` Real Moving Video Duration (`5–7` Downloaded Video Clips Law)**:
     - **Hard `> 85%` Moving Video Duration Floor**: Every Short (`9:16`) MUST have **`> 85.0%` of its total duration (`duration_ms`) as real moving video clips (`media_type in ("video", "hook_ready_video")`)**, equivalent to downloading **`5–7` distinct `.mp4` video clips** (for a 7-scene Short, **`6–7/7` scenes MUST be real moving videos**; at most `1` scene `< 15%` duration may be a static image).
     - **Zero Premature Race Cancellation & Zero Image Downgrade**: When racing Pexels/Pixabay and YouTube/Bilibili in parallel (`fetch_media_node`, `post_gather_rescue`, `refine_media_node`), **NEVER** set `yt_cancel_ev` unless the sibling branch has already returned a valid moving video (`win_res is not None`), **NEVER** abort a downloaded clip during Gemini Vision verification on `deadline_ts` just to reserve time for static images, and **NEVER** allow `can_downgrade_to_image` in `refine_media_node` when total moving video duration is `<= 85%`. Always keep Tier-3 Bilibili (`_try_bilibili_broll_fallback`) active even when `skip_stock_fallback=True`.
  3. **Rule 3 — US Geo-Routing & `8h45m` Wide-Spacing Schedule Law (Account for YouTube's `+1.5h–3h` Seed Wave Delay)**:
     - **Why `14:00–15:00 UTC` (`21:00–22:00 VN`) AND `21:00–21:45 UTC` (`04:00–04:45 AM VN`) Are FORBIDDEN for US Channels**: YouTube's Initial Seed Wave fires `+1.5h to +3.0h` after `publishAt`. Publishing at `14:00–15:00 UTC` hits Vietnam's bedtime scrolling peak (`22:00–23:59 VN`); publishing at `21:00–21:45 UTC` hits Vietnam's wake-up scrolling peak (`05:30–07:30 AM VN`, causing up to `81% VN` traffic).
     - **Locked Two-Slot US Schedule (`MIN_SPACING_SECONDS = 21600` / `8h 45m` Intra-Channel & `60m` Same-Gmail Stagger)**:
       - **US Slot #1 (`16:15–18:15 UTC` / `23:15–01:15 VN`, Base `17:15 UTC`)**: Seed wave fires at `01:00–04:15 AM VN` (100% Vietnam deep sleep) and hits US Lunch/Afternoon (`12:15 PM–04:00 PM EST/EDT`).
       - **US Slot #2 (`01:00–03:00 UTC` / `08:00–10:00 AM VN`, Base `02:00 UTC`)**: Separated by **`8 hours 45 minutes`** from Slot #1; seed wave hits **US Prime Night/Bedtime Peak (`08:00 PM–11:00 PM EST/EDT` & `05:00 PM–08:00 PM PST/PDT`)** while Vietnam is in morning work/school hours (`09:30–11:30 AM VN`).
       - **Cross-Channel Stagger**: Channels on the same Google Account (`account_1`, `acount_2`, `acccount4`) MUST be staggered by **`60–65 minutes`** (`Alternate History -60m`, `Psychology -30m`, `Paws & Hearts 0m`, `Mysteries EN +30m`, `Wild Odyssey +60m`; Brazil `Odisseia 13:20/20:15 UTC` vs `Mistérios 14:25/21:20 UTC`, `6h55m` apart).
  4. **Rule 4 — Anti-Collision, Comment Backfill & `50%` Auto-Thumbnail Hero-Frame Law**:
     - **Anti-Collision**: NEVER schedule a Long-Form (`16:9`) video and a Short (`9:16`) at the same timestamp on the same channel (enforce `>= 3.0 hours` / `10,800s` spacing).
     - **Comment Backfill**: Because YouTube Data API v3 blocks `commentThreads().insert` while a video is `private`/`scheduled`, always run `sync_channel_pinned_comments` / `ensure_pinned_comment` once scheduled videos go `public` so no public video stays at `0` comments. Lock `categoryId` to `'15'` (Wildlife/Pets) or `'22'` (Mysteries/History/Psychology) — NEVER `'27'` (`Education`).
     - **`50%` Midpoint Auto-Thumbnail Law (Unverified Channels)**: YouTube ALWAYS uses `2.jpg` (**the exact `50.0%` midpoint**) as `hqdefault.jpg` (`1.jpg` = `25%`, `3.jpg` = `75%`). Never stamp thumbnail text at `0.0s–0.5s`; for `16:9` Long-Form videos, always lock the sharpest close-up Hero Clip + a dark lower-third bar + Giant `88px` Two-Tone Banner (`Alignment=2`, `MarginV=48`) at `25%`, `50%`, and `75%`, and keep `16:9` titles `< 55–60 chars`.
  5. **Rule 5 — Zero Topic-Cloning, Model-Timeout Rotation & Historical/Flag Visual Integrity Law (`Anti-Flop Guardrail`)**:
     - **Canonical Channel ID & Subject-Prefix Deduplication**: Always normalize `channel_id` via `normalize_channel_id()` in `video_library.py` / `deduplication.py` so alias vs. handle mismatches (`ch_alternate_history` vs. `@alternatehistorywhatif-e2x`) never bypass duplicate checks. `check_idea_similarity` MUST compare both full tokens and the core **Subject/Entity prefix before `:`** (`similarity_threshold <= 0.40`). When static `cfg["topics"]` are exhausted, NEVER fall back blindly to `remaining[0]` or overwrite unrelated topics with static hardcoded fallback titles (`Vasili Arkhipov` / `Stanislav Petrov` / `Benjamin Franklin`), which triggers YouTube's **Repetitive Content `0-view` freeze**.
     - **Immediate Model Demotion on Read Timeout**: In `script_json_parser_mixin.py` and `KeyRotator`, ALWAYS call `mark_model_rate_limited(..., status_code=504)` on `Read timed out` so hung models are immediately demoted for `30s` instead of burning all retry attempts and falling back to static scripts.
     - **Zero Foreign-Flag / Era Anachronisms in B-Roll**: `VisualCurationAgent` (`media_vision_curator_mixin.py`) MUST score `< 5.0` (reject) any stock clip displaying a **foreign national flag/military insignia that contradicts the script's country/empire** (e.g., Turkish/Kyrgyz flags in a Soviet/US Cold War submarine story) or modern civilian tourists/cars in historical eras.
  6. **Rule 6 — 100% YAML Niche Profile Single Source of Truth (`config/niches/*.yaml` & `app/services/niche_manager.py`)**:
     - **YAML is the ONLY Source of Truth for Niche Topics, Script Forms & Visual Rules**: Every niche's topic forms (`script_blueprint.viral_topic_bank_2026`), hook formulas (`script_blueprint.hook_formulas`), narrative structure (`script_blueprint.narrative_structure`, `pacing`, `tone_of_voice`, `showrunner_prose_dna`), and visual/search strategies (`visual_aesthetics`, `llm_production_blueprint.video_search_strategy`, `visual_archetypes_and_tool_routing`, `abstract_to_physical_query_translations`, `strictly_forbidden_visuals`) are defined in **`config/niches/*.yaml`** (`psychology.yaml`, `wildlife_predators.yaml`, `deep_sea_earth_mysteries.yaml`, `alternate_history.yaml`, `animal_rescue.yaml`, `general.yaml`) and mapped in `config/channels.yaml`.
     - **Mandatory Dynamic Injection via `niche_manager.py`**: All agents (`ContentAgent`, `ViralIdeationMixin`, `ScriptCritic`, `LLMVisualDirector`, `VisualCurationAgent`, `VisualCritic`, `YouTubeSearchRanker`) and batch runners (`batch_producer.py`) MUST load niche rules dynamically via `app/services/niche_manager.py` (`get_niche`, `get_channel_niche`, `build_script_prompt_instructions`, `build_million_view_showrunner_prose_dna`, `build_llm_production_overview_prompt`, `build_video_search_strategy_prompt_block`).
     - **Zero Hardcoded Niche Prompts / `director_notes` in Python**: **NEVER** hardcode `director_notes`, static topic dictionaries (`cfg["topics"]`), or niche-specific script/visual rules in `.py` files, and **NEVER** force one niche's template (e.g. Wildlife `[Animal A] vs [Animal B]` combat) onto another niche (e.g. Psychology `Why Someone...` / `Why You...` dark-noir behavioral decoding). Whenever a niche's topic format, script structure, or visual rule needs tuning, update **`config/niches/<niche>.yaml`** — NOT Python code.

## 4. Mandatory System-Wide Root-Cause Bugfix & Anti-Overfitting (`systemic-root-cause-bugfix`)
- Whenever you debug, fix, or optimize any issue in the project, you **MUST** follow the [systemic-root-cause-bugfix](.agents/skills/systemic-root-cause-bugfix/SKILL.md) skill (incorporating GitHub's `obra/superpowers` `systematic-debugging`, `root-cause-tracing`, `defense-in-depth`, and Anti-Reward-Hacking guardrails):
  1. **Zero Test-Case Overfitting & Zero Keyword/Regex-Patching Policy (No Disguised Word Lists in Python)**: **NEVER** patch bugs by hardcoding topic/species/biome/weather/metaphor/cliché keyword lists in Python — whether as `if "snow" in ...`, `if "tiger" in ...`, disguised regex word lists (`re.compile(r"\b(word1|word2|...)\b")`), `re.sub` sentence rewrites, species/noun tuples, or synonym sets (`_syn_groups`) — just to pass a test case. Every fix must pass the **4-Domain Counterfactual Test** (generalizing across Wildlife, Ancient History, Dark Psychology, and Sci-Fi/Ocean Mysteries with ZERO domain-specific keywords in Python).
  2. **100% LLM System Prompt & YAML Niche Profile Authority (`config/niches/*.yaml` + `SceneContract`)**: Whenever AI search queries, scene visuals, topic forms, or script phrasing drift off-topic or misinterpret metaphors/pronouns, the root cause is in the **YAML Niche Profile (`config/niches/*.yaml`) or LLM System Prompt** — you **MUST** fix it in `config/niches/*.yaml` (injected via `niche_manager.py`) and the **System Prompts & JSON Contracts** (`ContentAgent` Showrunner directly outputting per-scene `subject_entity` and `search_queries` in `SceneContract`, `VisualDirector` prompt, `VisualCurationAgent` Vision prompt, and `VisualCritic` prompt), **NEVER** by writing Python regex filters, hardcoded `director_notes`, or keyword dictionaries in `.py` files.
  3. **Backward Root-Cause Tracing & Right-Layer Defense-in-Depth**: Trace bugs backward from the symptom to the upstream source (`FFmpeg` ⬅ `VisualCritic/RAG` ⬅ `StockFetcher` ⬅ `VisualCurationAgent` ⬅ `Query Normalization` ⬅ `System Prompt`) and fix the root cause across all parallel flows (`HookVideoNode`, `VisualCurationAgent` batch & single scene, video & photo curation, and topic-agnostic CV/FFmpeg gates).
